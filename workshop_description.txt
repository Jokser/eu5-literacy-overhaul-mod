[h1]Literacy Overhaul[/h1]

In vanilla, literacy is one number for everyone: a printing press or a new religion teaches peasants as much as priests. This mod gives every class its own literacy, so a medieval country has a learned elite and an illiterate countryside, as it historically did. Research depends on how well educated your elite is, and the rest of the game is adjusted so that the lower averages do not break it.

[h2]Class-stratified literacy[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Nobles, clergy and burghers[/b] can reach high literacy. [b]Peasants, laborers and soldiers[/b] stay low until mass schooling: printed primers, Protestant Bible reading, secular education.
[*]Every advance, building, law, reform, privilege, religion, holy site and modifier that gave literacy to "everyone" has been reviewed: academies, libraries and courts educate the elite, schools and cheap books reach the common people.
[/list]

[h2]Print and mass reading[/h2]
[list]
[*]The [b]printing press[/b] educates the elite. [b]Printed Primers[/b], a cheap follow-up advance, bring reading to the common people.
[*]Mass print depends on [b]print culture[/b]: Catholic and Protestant Europe and the woodblock cultures of East Asia get the primers early, everyone else only in the age of revolutions. [b]Madrasas[/b] educate the ulema, not the villages.
[*][b]Newspapers[/b] educate the elite, the [b]Popular Press[/b] the common people. Late schooling: Catholic [b]Teaching Orders[/b], [b]Enlightened Pedagogy[/b] and [b]secular education[/b].
[/list]

[h2]Religions and cultures[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Catholic[/b] clergy are highly literate. [b]Orthodox[/b] clergy rely on monastic book culture until the [b]Stoglav[/b] parish schools and [b]Kyiv-Mohyla learning[/b].
[*][b]Protestants[/b] are the most literate churches: [b]Sola Scriptura[/b], [b]translated Bibles[/b] and [b]personal religious education[/b] bring the Bible to the common people, and Protestant towns build [b]Latin schools[/b], the counterpart of the Jesuit colleges.
[*][b]Islam[/b], Hindu, Jain, Theravada, Armenian, Chinese and Maya learning all have their own profiles.
[/list]

[h2]Learned Elite: a new research bonus[/h2]
[list]
[*]Vanilla research uses the [i]average[/i] literacy, which is mostly the peasants'. A new [b]Learned Elite[/b] modifier adds research from the literacy of your [b]nobles (40%), clergy (35%) and burghers (25%)[/b].
[*]Each institution gives +4% research instead of +5% and average control counts half. Simulated on saves from 1450 to 1800, no age becomes noticeably faster or slower.
[*]The [b]liturgical language[/b] counts half as much for research (up to +10% instead of +20%): Arabic, always the most powerful language, let every Muslim country research as fast as France.
[/list]

[h2]Russia: Modernization on the Western Model[/h2]
[list]
[*]The [b]Great Embassy[/b] unlocks a new Russian reform: free (it adds its own slot), [b]+15% research[/b] and +5 literacy for nobles and burghers, at the cost of clergy satisfaction. The AI now prefers the Great Embassy over other expeditions.
[/list]

[h2]Keeping the rest of the game intact[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Institution spread[/b] is compensated: about the vanilla pace in Europe, slower where literacy was historically low.
[*]Books and paper demand, peasant promotion, the Royal Society, the revolution disasters and other literacy checks are adjusted to the lower averages.
[*]The AI climbs the mass-education ladder (parish schools, guild apprenticeships, Protestant Bible reading, secular schools), picks elite education by the strongest estate and chooses historically fitting press laws.
[/list]

[h2]Universities[/h2]
[list]
[*]A university needs a developed town (30+) with highly literate clergy (85+) and burghers (40+). Every area has room for one; [b]a second and third[/b] (one per 400 development) open with [b]Confessionalism[/b] and the [b]Scientific Revolution[/b]. About 20 universities in Italy and 45 in Germany by 1800.
[*]Universities give a big literacy boost to the upper classes, [b]+2% research each[/b] (up to five) and faster institution growth. They cost more to build and maintain.
[*]Loopholes that built universities anywhere are closed; historical flavour events still found theirs.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Most changes replace single entries (REPLACE_OR_CREATE), so mods that do not touch literacy are compatible. Eleven files are replaced whole (institution spread, the institution files, three events files, the Grand Embassy, two parliament issue files); other mods replacing them conflict.

[b]Ruthenia and Steppe Fix (RSF):[/b] use [b]Literacy overhaul - RSF compatibility[/b], loaded after both mods. Literacy overhaul itself does not need RSF.

Based in part on the literacy changes of Flavorium Universalis Balance.
